Question 2
⏱️ 23s

What is Reach?

🎉 Spot on! Reach measures unique individuals.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Reach

The total number of unique people who saw your ad at least once.

Unique Individuals vs. Multiple Views

👩 Priya Saw it 3 times Counts as 1
👨 Aman Saw it 1 time Counts as 1
👦 Rohit Saw it 2 times Counts as 1
Total Unique Viewers = 3 People (Reach)

Reach Calculation Check

In the scenario above with 6 impressions across Priya, Aman, and Rohit, what is the Reach?

A) 3 Unique People
B) 6 Total Deliveries
C) 1 Account
D) 5 Contacts
💡
Remember: Reach counts people, not views. Each unique person is counted only once regardless of ad frequency.
Question 3
⏱️ 23s

What is Frequency?

🎉 Excellent! Frequency = Impressions ÷ Reach.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Frequency

The average number of times each person saw your ad.

Frequency =
Impressions Reach
Our Scenario: 6 Impressions ÷ 3 Reach = 2.0 Frequency
1.0 - 2.0: Fresh Audience
2.0 - 4.0: High Recall
5.0+: Potential Ad Fatigue

Frequency Check

If an ad achieves 1,000 Impressions across 500 Reach, what is the ad frequency?

A) 0.5
B) 2.0 (1,000 ÷ 500)
C) 5.0
D) 500
💡
Remember: High frequency can mean users are fatigued with your ad; low frequency may mean they haven't noticed it yet.

What is the difference between Clicks (all) and Unique Clicks (all)?

🎉 Brilliant! Unique Clicks deduplicates multiple clicks from the same person.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Unique Clicks (all)

The number of unique people who performed any click interaction.

Deduplication in Action

👩 Priya Liked + Clicked Link (2 clicks) Counts as 1 Unique Clicker
👨 Aman Commented + Expanded Photo (2 clicks) Counts as 1 Unique Clicker
👦 Rohit Clicked Link (1 click) Counts as 1 Unique Clicker
Total Clicks (all): 5 Unique Clicks (all): 3

Unique Clicks Check

A) 5
B) 3
C) 15
D) 2
💡
Remember: Just as Reach is to Impressions, Unique Clicks is to Clicks (all).

What does Landing Page Views count?

🎉 Great job! A landing page view requires the website to successfully load.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Landing Page Views

The user didn't just click — the destination page actually opened and loaded.

The Loading Drop-off (Bounce) Reality

1 3 Link Clicks

3 users tapped the ad link

➔
2 Network / Load Time

Page loading...

➔
3 2 Loaded Views

1 user bounced before load!

⚠️ If Link Clicks are much higher than Landing Page Views, your site is too slow!

Load Check

3 people clicked your ad link. 2 waited for the page to load; 1 closed the tab immediately. How many Landing Page Views are recorded?

A) 3
B) 2
C) 1
D) 0
💡
Remember: A Landing Page View requires the Meta Pixel to fire on your site. Slow loading sites lose massive traffic here.

How is Click-Through Rate (CTR) calculated?

🎉 Spot on! CTR = (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Click-Through Rate (CTR)

The percentage of impressions that led to a click.

CTR =
Clicks Impressions
× 100
Benchmark Guide: Average Feed Ad CTR: 0.90% - 1.50%
0.5% (Low) 1.2% (Good) 2.5%+ (Outstanding)

💡
Remember: CTR tells you how effective your creative and copy are at stopping thumbs and generating interest.

How is Conversion Rate (CVR) calculated?

🎉 Spot on! CVR = (Conversions ÷ Total Clicks) × 100.
✈️
Concept Deep Dive

Conversion Rate (CVR)

The percentage of clicks that turned into valuable conversions.

CVR =
Conversions Clicks
× 100
Funnel Diagnostic: High CTR + Low CVR = Landing Page / Offer Problem!
Creative Stops Scroll (CTR)
Landing Page Convinces User (CVR)

CVR Calculation Check

Remember: CVR evaluates the quality of your website and offer, while CTR evaluates the ad creative.
Question 11
⏱️ 23s

How do CPC and CPA evaluate campaign efficiency?

🎉 Spot on! CPC measures traffic cost, while CPA measures actual customer acquisition cost.
✈️
Final Concept

CPC (Cost per Click) and CPA (Cost per Action)

CPC is what we pay to get one click; CPA is what we pay to acquire one conversion.

1

CPC (Cost per Click)

Cost paid to Meta for every click on our ad unit.

CPC = Spend ÷ Clicks
If spend is ₹500 for 100 clicks: ₹5 per click
2

CPA (Cost per Action)

Cost paid to acquire one conversion (desired business action).

CPA = Spend ÷ Conversions
If spend is ₹1,000 for 20 conversions: ₹50 per conversion
💡
REMEMBER: Everything in the user journey comes at a cost. Manage your funnel to keep CPA profitable!
